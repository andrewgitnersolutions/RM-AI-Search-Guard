AI-generatedWhy do plants look green under sunlight?

Chlorophyll absorbs red and blue wavelengths while reflecting green wavelengths back to our eyes.

What is the balanced chemical equation for photosynthesis?

Six molecules of carbon dioxide plus six molecules of water yield one glucose molecule and six oxygen molecules.

Where in a plant cell does the Calvin cycle occur?

The light-independent reactions of the Calvin cycle take place in the chloroplast stroma surrounding the thylakoids.

What is the difference between light-dependent and light-independent reactions?

Light-dependent reactions capture solar energy in ATP and NADPH, while the Calvin cycle fixes carbon into sugar.

Why is chlorophyll a essential for oxygenic photosynthesis?

Chlorophyll a acts as the primary reaction-center pigment transferring excited electrons into the electron transport chain.

How does carbon dioxide enter the leaves of terrestrial plants?

Carbon dioxide diffuses through microscopic stomatal pores flanked by specialized guard cells on the leaf epidermis.
